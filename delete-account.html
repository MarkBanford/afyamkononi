Account and data deletion

Request deletion of your Afyamkononi account

Afyamkononi is operated by Nurses at your doorstep in Kenya. You can request deletion of your Afyamkononi account and associated personal information without opening the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Email nursesatyourdoorstep@gmail.com with the subject Afyamkononi Account Deletion Request.
  2. State the email address or phone number associated with your account. Do not include passwords, payment PINs, or medical details.
  3. The support team may contact you to verify that the account belongs to you before processing the request.

Email an account deletion request

Delete from within the app

You can also use the account/data deletion option in your Afyamkononi profile.

What happens to your data?

Following a verified request, Afyamkononi will assess deletion of your account and associated personal information. Some records, such as healthcare or transaction records, may need to be retained where Kenyan law or applicable record-keeping obligations require it. See the Privacy Policy for more information.

Omba kufutwa kwa akaunti yako

Unaweza kuomba kufutwa kwa akaunti yako ya Afyamkononi bila kufungua programu.

  1. Tuma barua pepe kwa nursesatyourdoorstep@gmail.com ukiomba kufuta akaunti.
  2. Taja barua pepe au nambari ya simu iliyotumika kusajili akaunti. Usitume nenosiri, PIN ya malipo au taarifa za afya.
  3. Timu inaweza kukuomba uthibitishe umiliki wa akaunti kabla ya kuifuta.
