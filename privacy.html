1. Introduction

Welcome to Afyamkononi. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal health and demographic information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, which connects patients in Kenya with nurse- and clinician-led symptom checking and health guidance. By downloading, accessing, or using our application, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Information We Collect

To provide accurate symptom checking, clinical guidance, and seamless service delivery within Kenya, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, phone number (required for M-Pesa transaction processing and account verification), and email address.
  • Health and Medical Information: Information you voluntarily provide regarding your current symptoms, medical history, triage assessments, and health inquiries shared with our clinical team.
  • Transaction and Financial Data: Payment details processed securely via mobile money integration (such as Safaricom M-Pesa, IntaSend, or Pesapal) when paying for consultations or premium services. We do not store your raw mobile money PINs.
  • Technical and Usage Data: Device information, IP address, operating system, app usage logs, and diagnostic data to improve app performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To deliver professional nurse- and clinician-led symptom assessments and health guidance.
  • To facilitate secure payment processing via Kenyan payment gateways (M-Pesa / Pesapal / IntaSend).
  • To maintain user accounts, verify identity, and communicate important health updates or appointment details.
  • To comply with local healthcare regulations and data protection standards in Kenya (including the Data Protection Act, 2019).
  • To monitor and improve the safety, security, and functionality of our application.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Healthcare Professionals: With licensed nurses and clinicians directly involved in reviewing your symptoms and providing your health guidance.
  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party vendors (such as cloud hosting providers and payment processors like Safaricom or payment gateways) who assist us in operating the app, strictly under confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: If required to do so by Kenyan law, court order, or government regulation.

5. Data Security and Retention

We implement robust technical and organisational security measures — including data encryption in transit and at rest — to protect your sensitive health records from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We retain your health and account records only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with medical record-keeping standards.

6. Your Data Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Access and request a copy of the personal and health data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request the deletion of your account and personal data, subject to legal or clinical record retention requirements.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for use by adults, or by minors under the direct supervision of a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal health data from children without appropriate parental consent.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at: Email: nursesatyourdoorstep@gmail.com Location: Nairobi, Kenya

1. Utangulizi

Karibu Afyamkononi. Tunaheshimu faragha yako na tumejitolea kulinda taarifa zako binafsi za afya na za kidemografia. Sera hii ya Faragha inaeleza jinsi tunavyokusanya, kutumia, kufichua na kulinda taarifa zako unapotumia programu yetu ya simu, inayowaunganisha wagonjwa nchini Kenya na huduma za kukagua dalili na ushauri wa afya zinazoongozwa na wauguzi na matabibu. Kwa kupakua, kufungua au kutumia programu yetu, unakubali masharti ya Sera hii ya Faragha. Iwapo hukubali, tafadhali usitumie programu. Toleo hili la Kiswahili limetolewa ili kukusaidia kuelewa. Iwapo kutakuwa na tofauti yoyote kati ya toleo la Kiingereza na la Kiswahili, toleo la Kiingereza ndilo litakalotumika.

2. Taarifa Tunazokusanya

Ili kutoa ukaguzi sahihi wa dalili, ushauri wa kitabibu na huduma bora ndani ya Kenya, tunaweza kukusanya aina zifuatazo za taarifa:

  • Taarifa za Utambulisho Binafsi: Jina, nambari ya simu (inahitajika kwa malipo ya M-Pesa na kuthibitisha akaunti), na anwani ya barua pepe.
  • Taarifa za Afya na Matibabu: Taarifa unazotoa kwa hiari kuhusu dalili zako za sasa, historia ya matibabu, tathmini za uchunguzi wa awali (triage), na maswali ya afya unayoshiriki na timu yetu ya kitabibu.
  • Data ya Miamala na Fedha: Maelezo ya malipo yanayochakatwa kwa usalama kupitia huduma za pesa kwa simu (kama Safaricom M-Pesa, IntaSend au Pesapal) unapolipia mashauriano au huduma maalum. Hatuhifadhi PIN yako ya pesa kwa simu.
  • Data ya Kiufundi na Matumizi: Taarifa za kifaa, anwani ya IP, mfumo wa uendeshaji, kumbukumbu za matumizi ya programu, na data ya uchunguzi wa hitilafu ili kuboresha utendaji wa programu.

3. Jinsi Tunavyotumia Taarifa Zako

Tunatumia taarifa tunazokusanya kwa madhumuni yafuatayo:

  • Kutoa tathmini za kitaalamu za dalili na ushauri wa afya unaoongozwa na wauguzi na matabibu.
  • Kuwezesha malipo salama kupitia njia za malipo za Kenya (M-Pesa / Pesapal / IntaSend).
  • Kusimamia akaunti za watumiaji, kuthibitisha utambulisho, na kuwasiliana nawe kuhusu taarifa muhimu za afya au miadi.
  • Kutii kanuni za afya za ndani na viwango vya ulinzi wa data nchini Kenya (ikiwemo Sheria ya Ulinzi wa Data, 2019).
  • Kufuatilia na kuboresha usalama, ulinzi na utendaji wa programu yetu.

4. Kushiriki na Kufichua Data

Hatuuzi, hatukodishi wala hatubadilishani data yako binafsi au ya afya. Tunaweza kushiriki taarifa katika hali chache zifuatazo pekee:

  • Wataalamu wa Afya: Pamoja na wauguzi na matabibu waliosajiliwa wanaohusika moja kwa moja katika kukagua dalili zako na kukupa ushauri wa afya.
  • Watoa Huduma: Pamoja na wachuuzi wengine wanaoaminika (kama watoa huduma za kuhifadhi data mtandaoni na wachakataji wa malipo kama Safaricom au njia nyingine za malipo) wanaotusaidia kuendesha programu, chini ya makubaliano madhubuti ya usiri.
  • Kutii Sheria: Iwapo tutahitajika kufanya hivyo na sheria ya Kenya, amri ya mahakama au kanuni za serikali.

5. Usalama na Uhifadhi wa Data

Tunatumia hatua madhubuti za kiufundi na za kiutawala — ikiwemo usimbaji fiche wa data inaposafirishwa na inapohifadhiwa — ili kulinda kumbukumbu zako nyeti za afya dhidi ya kufikiwa, kubadilishwa, kufichuliwa au kuharibiwa bila idhini. Tunahifadhi kumbukumbu zako za afya na za akaunti kwa muda unaohitajika tu ili kutimiza madhumuni yaliyoelezwa katika sera hii au kutii viwango vya utunzaji wa kumbukumbu za matibabu.

6. Haki Zako Kuhusu Data

Chini ya Sheria ya Ulinzi wa Data ya Kenya, 2019, una haki ya:

  • Kufikia na kuomba nakala ya data binafsi na ya afya tunayohifadhi kukuhusu.
  • Kuomba kurekebishwa kwa data isiyo sahihi au isiyokamilika.
  • Kuomba kufutwa kwa akaunti yako na data yako binafsi, kwa kuzingatia mahitaji ya kisheria au ya kitabibu ya kuhifadhi kumbukumbu.
  • Kuondoa idhini yako ya kuchakata data wakati wowote.

7. Faragha ya Watoto

Programu yetu imekusudiwa kutumiwa na watu wazima, au watoto chini ya usimamizi wa moja kwa moja wa mzazi au mlezi halali. Hatukusanyi kwa makusudi data ya afya ya watoto bila idhini inayofaa ya mzazi.

8. Mabadiliko ya Sera Hii ya Faragha

Tunaweza kusasisha Sera hii ya Faragha mara kwa mara. Mabadiliko yoyote yatachapishwa kwenye ukurasa huu pamoja na "Tarehe ya Kuanza" mpya. Tunakuhimiza kukagua sera hii mara kwa mara.
